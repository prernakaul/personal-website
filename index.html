Hello!
I’m Prerna, a Group Product Manager at Panasonic, leading AI agent platforms. I build products at the intersection of LLMs, data, and infrastructure. Most recently, I’ve been focused on safe, compliant AI agents for healthcare and consumer wellness.
Previously, at Moderna, I helped launch 750+ FDA-aligned AI agents and a multimodal vaccine assistant that supported a major oncology partnership. Before that, at Amazon, I worked on reinforcement learning and data quality for AWS Titan, and built ML Ops and experimentation services on AWS SageMaker used by thousands of scientists.
I have an engineering degree from Georgia Tech and an MBA from Rotman (University of Toronto), and I’ve shipped products across healthcare, consumer, and B2B enterprise.

Earlier in my career, I:

  • Launched Walmart’s Scan & Go mobile experience across 400+ Canadian stores at Walmart Labs
  • Shipped the flagship Points Loyalty Wallet at Points.com
  • Outside of work, I love speaking, writing, and coaching. I’ve given 10+ talks on machine learning, platform product management, and careers in tech, I’m a startup mentor with First Round Fast Track, and was recently featured in Lenny’s Newsletter.
